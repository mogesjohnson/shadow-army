# Unit M1, round 1, batch M1-V3 (death and containment)

## R-M1-9

- severity: P1
  type: gap
  location: PO-1 (docs/spec/v2.md:168); PO-7 (:186); M1-AC4 (:549-553)
  claim: Nothing requires a soldier's process tree to end when the post office itself dies abruptly, and no M1 test kills the post office. An orphaned soldier can run on with no supervisor, guard or log, and no way to stop it.
  scenario: The post office dies while Iron (claude, autonomy full) is mid-turn: the OOM killer, Task Manager, a native crash, or taken down with Claude Code's own process on Windows. PO-1's cleanup runs only "when a channel closes or restarts", which a dead post office can't do. Iron's input ends; nothing documented says that stops its turn (NEEDS-PROBE). claude then stays open up to 30 min for its background commands and takes one more turn. Nobody reads its output, nothing guards or logs it, and the owner has no pane or command to stop it.
  evidence: :168 "When a channel closes or restarts, the post office ends the CLI's whole process tree". :186 names "its process containment (PO-1)", which no requirement defines. 5.1 F (:477) kills a CLI, never the post office. M3-AC12 (:631) kills the post office but checks only the message state. The vendor docs are cited in the shutdown finding. Codex's draft does contain it: in C:\Users\johns\projects\AIConnections-m1\src\v2\processTree.ts, the Linux guardian ends the process group when its IPC channel closes, "even when the post office is killed without running finally"; Windows uses a kill-on-close job and macOS a coalition. C:\Users\johns\projects\AIConnections-m1\src\v2\client.ts notes "Windows' default child job dies with its launcher before guard finalization can run". NEEDS-PROBE: whether claude exits once its output pipe breaks.
  fix: "PO-1: Each soldier's process tree is contained so that it ends within N seconds of the post office's own process ending, however that happens, a kill or a crash included." 5.1 F and M1-AC4 add: "the post office killed mid-turn, with a fake CLI that left a child running: both are gone within N seconds."

## R-F-10

- severity: P1
  type: gap
  location: §3 Architecture (docs/spec/v2.md:82-87); HOST-2 (:94); PO-1 (:164, :168); M3-AC12 (:631); §5.1 F (:476-491)
  claim: The spec never says what happens when the post office itself dies. It doesn't say who notices or who restarts it. It doesn't say what becomes of the soldiers' CLIs, their open turns and guards, the queued and delivered messages, or the folder lock, or what the owner sees.
  scenario: Three soldiers are mid-turn and two briefs are queued when the post office is killed (OOM or a crash). Reading A, as in Codex's draft: every later `send` throws "post office is closed", and the lock stays until the owner deletes it by hand. Reading B: the mod restarts it with a new secret. Either way, five things are undefined: (1) whether the CLIs die or run on with no guard (a Linux subreaper's orphans go to init); (2) whether the cut-short turns are ever finished as PO-8 says, when the guard's baseline was only in memory; (3) whether "queued" records become "not delivered"; (4) what the pane shows: its spinners keep turning on stale "working" states, and no toast fires; (5) M3-AC12 tests behavior "after a restart", but no requirement defines a restart.
  evidence: HOST-2 starts the post office only "the first time the army is used in a session". PO-1:168 covers only "When a channel closes or restarts". The fault level has "a CLI killed mid-turn" but no post office kill. Codex's draft: client.ts:76 throws "post office is closed"; server.ts:33 says "remove its stale lock after checking the recorded PID".
  fix: Add to §3 and PO-1: "If the post office exits unexpectedly, the mod shows the army as down (strip, status entry, one transcript line). On start, a post office: reclaims a lock whose pid and start time match no living process; finishes each turn its records show open (PO-8: guard against a baseline kept on disk, then audit, then message interrupted); ends each queued message as not delivered, reason 'post office stopped'. Nothing the CLIs started outlives the post office (PO-1)." The restart policy is a decision: restart on the next use, or ask the owner. Add fault cases: the post office killed mid-turn, with a queue, and with an approval open. Add an integration case with a fake mod host.

## R-M1-5

- severity: P2
  type: defect
  location: PO-1 (docs/spec/v2.md:166,168); M1-AC4 (docs/spec/v2.md:553); §5.1 fault tests (docs/spec/v2.md:479)
  claim: PO-1 claims process-tree termination means "nothing the CLI started keeps running," which is not true against a hostile child and is a weaker guarantee than the carried-over 2.0 SAFE-11 itself admitted, giving a persistence path.
  scenario: A prompt-injected soldier double-forks/`setsid`s, or registers a user systemd unit, a cron/at job, a launchd agent, or a Windows scheduled task/service. On channel close PO-1 ends the process tree, but a process re-parented to PID 1 or owned by the init/scheduler survives — the soldier persists past session end and hides activity. The fault test "a CLI that leaves a child process running" (479) and M1-AC4's orphan-child bullet (553) exercise only a cooperative in-tree child, never an escaping one.
  evidence: PO-1:168 "the post office ends the CLI's whole process tree, so nothing the CLI started keeps running." The carried-over predecessor, 2.0 SAFE-11 (legacy/spec/shadow-army-2.0.md:1049), states the opposite as a residual: "Vendor daemons outside the group (F34) are a stated residual risk." v2 dropped that admission while strengthening the claim. (D-3 decides how the containment helper ships; this finding is about the over-stated guarantee and the untested adversarial case, not the shipping mechanism.)
  fix: Soften PO-1 to a best-effort guarantee, name the residual (a child that re-parents to init or registers with the OS scheduler/init can survive), and rely on the checkout guard / a survivor check to detect it; add a fault test for a child that escapes the tree.

## R-M1-14

- severity: P2
  type: gap
  location: PO-1 (docs/spec/v2.md:165); M1-AC4 (:551)
  claim: "Restarted at most three times" gives no time window, no delay between attempts, and no rule for a start that fails at once. The limit is used up at once by a bad start, and never resets in a long session.
  scenario: (1) `claude` rejects a flag and exits at once: three restarts in under a second, then "down". (2) In one long Claude session, Kaisel crashes once on Monday, once on Tuesday and once on Wednesday. Thursday's crash leaves it down, although each crash was a single event (Codex's draft resets the count only when the session is reopened). (3) Does r (reconnect) reset the count? Does a restart that itself fails to start count?
  evidence: :165 "A channel that crashes is restarted at most three times; after that it is down until the owner reconnects it." The headless docs: "If you pass an invalid flag, Claude Code reports the error to stderr before the run starts." In Codex's draft, C:\Users\johns\projects\AIConnections-m1\src\v2\postOffice.ts does `session.restarts++` and never resets it.
  fix: "PO-1: A crashed channel is restarted after 1 s, then 5 s, then 30 s. A fourth crash within 10 minutes of the first leaves it down. A start that ends before the CLI's first event counts as a crash. A reconnect, or 10 minutes without a crash, resets the count." (Values: decision.)

## R-M1-15

- severity: P2
  type: ambiguity
  location: PO-1 (docs/spec/v2.md:166); CH-1 (:219); 5.1 F "an expired login" (:488)
  claim: An error that the CLI reports just before it exits is both a CH-1 error and a PO-1 crash. So an expired login is restarted three times and ends as "down" instead of "log in".
  scenario: The owner's claude login expires. claude reports `authentication_failed` and exits non-zero, as documented for `-p` runs (NEEDS-PROBE for stream-json input mode). PO-1 counts a crash and restarts the channel. Each restart fails the same way, and after three the soldier is "down". HOST-6 has both "log in" and "down"; the text doesn't say which one shows, or whether a login error is restarted at all, and the F test has no expected outcome.
  evidence: :166 "An error the CLI reports while it keeps running (CH-1) never restarts it" says nothing about an error followed by an exit. https://code.claude.com/docs/en/headless: "When a failure happens inside the run, such as missing authentication, Claude Code prints the failure as the result on stdout", and the run exits non-zero. https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/typescript: the assistant message's `error` includes 'authentication_failed', and the error result carries `startup_failure_reason`.
  fix: "PO-1: When the CLI reports a login needed (CH-1) and then exits, the channel isn't restarted: the soldier shows 'log in' until the owner reconnects. Any other error the CLI reports before it exits counts as a crash, and keeps its kind and words."

## R-M1-19

- severity: P2
  type: gap
  location: PO-1 (docs/spec/v2.md:166); 5.1 F "a slow first token" (:487)
  claim: A CLI that stays alive but sends nothing is neither a crash nor an error, and no limit or state covers it. A hung soldier shows "working" forever, and a watchdog a builder adds can turn a slow start into a crash loop.
  scenario: (1) Iron's claude waits on an MCP server that never answers. No event comes for an hour, and the soldier shows working. In M1 there is no stop (CH-10 is M2, MSG-9 is M3) and no timeout. (2) The reverse: with `--mcp-config` (added in M3 by PO-4), claude may wait up to MCP_TIMEOUT, 30 s by default, before its first turn. Codex's draft fails the channel if claude doesn't answer `initialize` within 30 s, so a slow but healthy start is restarted three times and ends down. The F test "a slow first token" has no expected outcome.
  evidence: :166 defines a crash. :487 lists the test with no result. https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cli-reference, `--mcp-config`: "Claude Code waits for still-pending servers to connect before running the first turn, up to the MCP_TIMEOUT startup timeout, 30 seconds by default". In Codex's draft, control() in C:\Users\johns\projects\AIConnections-m1\src\v2\claude.ts calls failTransport after 30 s.
  fix: "PO-1: A CLI that is alive but silent is not a crash, and the post office never ends one for silence. A start that has produced no event within MCP_TIMEOUT plus N seconds has failed, and counts as a crash." 5.1 F gives "a slow first token" this outcome. (Whether, and from when, the owner is shown the silence: decision.)
