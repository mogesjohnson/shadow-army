- **M1-AC1** The post office starts with the first use of the army, starts again at the first use after it has shut down, and stops with the session. It binds only the loopback address and refuses `0.0.0.0` with an error. Tests of the lock, the lease and the watch use a fake claude, a fake monotonic clock and a fake `$` (PO-1, PO-2, PO-7, HOST-2, SEC-1):
  - its lock holds its pid, start time and port. A lock with a dead pid, one with a living pid and another start time, and an empty one are each replaced, and the log says so. A live lock refuses the start with its message and is left as it was. Two starts racing on one stale lock end with one post office;
  - with a renewal every 10 seconds, through an hour, the post office runs on, and its log has no line for any renewal. At the first check 30 seconds after the last renewal, it logs why and when, closes each session through the fake's exit, releases its lock and exits. A fake that ignores its exit has its process tree ended 10 seconds later;
  - the last renewal comes 9 seconds before a step of an hour between two checks, as after a sleep. The lease ends 16 seconds after the step, not at once. A renewal 10 seconds after the step keeps it, and so does one handled before the first check after the step. Moving the wall clock an hour either way changes nothing;
  - on Linux and macOS, a real post office stopped with SIGSTOP for 40 seconds and then continued, while the mod renews, keeps its lease;
  - a post office started without a session id refuses to start. A renewal with another session id, one from an earlier start, and any renewal after the lease ended, is refused with its reason and renews nothing;
  - while nothing reads its standard output, on each of the three systems (VER-2), it goes on taking renewals and the lease still ends on time; past 16 MiB of unwritten events it stops reading the fake's output, and reads it again once the events have been read;
  - when the process that started it ends, the post office starts the same shutdown within 2 seconds, without waiting for the lease. A new process with that pid but another start time counts as ended;
  - the mod renews every 10 seconds, with at most one renewal waiting, and skips those that fall due meanwhile; a failed renewal stops nothing, and one unanswered after 5 seconds no longer counts as waiting. At `session.end` it stops renewing, then sends the release, and the post office shuts down even when the release fails;
  - a fake that is mid-turn at the shutdown, ignores the end of its input and keeps a child running has both ended within 10 seconds; nothing restarts it, a crash restart that falls due after the shutdown started starts nothing, and the post office releases its lock and exits;
  - the post office is killed, and in a second case ended by its lease, while the fake mod lives. Each time the fake and its child are gone within 10 seconds, Claude's transcript gets one line with the reason, and the next use starts a new post office with a new secret;
  - a reloaded fake mod starts its own post office at the next use once the old lock is stale, and Claude's transcript gets one line.
