=== NEW
    A turn the CLI starts by itself after its message's work is over (PO-8) has no message to wait for. It starts from the state that the post office saves, in the same way, once the message's own turn has been reverted. An edit that the owner makes in that worktree after that point is the owner's to keep or revert (D-24):
    - when the turn's first event comes, the post office saves the bytes and mode of each audited path that then differs from that start state, beside it and in the same way, with the copy's SHA-256 hash in the log line that records the event (PO-5);
    - when the turn is over, and before its revert, those paths go to the owner on a card, each with its diff from the start state, as in `Iron took a turn by itself after a review · 2 files changed before it began · y keep as yours, n revert`. y keeps each as it was at the first event: the copy becomes part of the start state, and the revert brings the path back to it. n reverts them with the turn. y and n are the attribution card's keys (ARM-6), and the soldier's queue is held until the owner answers (MSG-3);
    - a change made after the first event is the turn's, and is reverted like any other;
    - with no such path, no card shows.

    The card lists what changed before the post office read the turn's first event, so a write the turn made in that moment can be on it, and y keeps it (Appendix D, residual 11). A card still open when the post office ends leaves the revert pending, as below, and the next post office shows the card again before that revert runs.
>>> END
