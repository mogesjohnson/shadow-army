D-2 lease timing: discrete-event simulation (fake clock, integer microseconds, seeded)
seed 20261014; 1000 phases per stall/death case, 20 eight-hour runs, 12 sleep phases per sleep case
Timing A: renew 10 s, expire 30 s.  Timing B: renew 5 s, expire 20 s.  Delivery delay of each renewal: uniform 1-50 ms.
K1 naive/sleep counted (= Codex lease.ts)   K2 monotonic/sleep not counted   K3 suspend-aware (1 s tick, gap > 3 ticks moves the deadline)
Variants: K1L lenient renew | K3S K3 + strict renew | K3N K3 on a clock that stops in sleep | K0 control, wall-clock lease
