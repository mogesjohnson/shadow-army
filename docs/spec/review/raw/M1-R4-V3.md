  reason:

- id: R-M1-222
  verdict: CONFIRMED
  severity: P2 (kept). The stopped turn doesn't read "nothing": under :509 it reads $0.00 with 0 tokens by model. The cost is under-counted in SES-7 and HOST-15, and in §5.1 L's budget a stopped run counts $0 against the $2 limit instead of its tokens against 1,000,000.
  evidence:
    - The probe, raw/probes-claude.md:48-65, claude 2.1.312:
      - Result 1: `total_cost_usd` 0.0728679.
      - Result 2, after the interrupt: `total_cost_usd` 0.0728679 (unchanged), `modelUsage` identical to result 1's, and `usage` not zero ("in 2, out 0, cr 30056, cc 13150, `iterations: []`").
      - Result 3 = result 1 plus turn 3 only.
      - The probe's own limit: "interrupted-turn usage is not reliable".
      - probes.md:17.
    - The spec: v2@4f481fc.md:511 needs all three fields at zero, which never happens after a stop, so its "after a stop" clause never applies. :509 then gives the stopped turn total less baseline = 0. :512 and :513 don't fire either: the total is not zero and not below the baseline.
    - :983 lists only "a claude turn after a crash" as "not reported".
    - ledger.md:297 (R-M1-75) left the stop clause waiting on P-11; this probe answers it.
    - Not covered anywhere else: grep for "stopped turn", "after a stop", `total_cost_usd` and "not reported". M1-AC3 (:1063) and M2-AC6 (:1182) have no case for a stopped claude turn's figures. §5.1 C (:921) already records "a turn stopped through the CLI's own interrupt, then the next message", so the frames exist.
  reason: claude adds nothing to its running totals for a stopped turn, so CH-2's zero test never matches and the difference rule reports a false $0. The figure should read "not reported", taken from the stop the adapter itself sent, not from the totals.
  fix:
    - CH-2 :511, replace with:
      ```
      - A result whose `usage`, `total_cost_usd` and `modelUsage` are all zero, as after a crash, reports nothing. That turn's input and cache are summed the same way, and its output and cost read "not reported".
      - A result that ends a turn the adapter stopped (CH-10, →M2) leaves out what the stop cut short: claude adds none of it to `total_cost_usd` or `modelUsage`, which go on growing from the result before, and the result's `usage` is not reliable (probe P-11, claude 2.1.312). So that turn's cost and output read "not reported", even where its totals grew, and its input and cache are summed over its main-loop assistant messages as above. Its totals are the next result's baseline, so the next turn's figures are its own.
      ```
    - §5.1 L :983: change "such as a claude turn after a crash (CH-2)" to "such as a claude turn after a crash or a stop (CH-2)".
    - New AC case, added to M2-AC6 (:1182): "For claude, replaying the recorded stop, the stopped turn's cost and output read "not reported", its input and cache are its main-loop ones, and the next turn's figures are that turn's alone, its result less the stopped turn's (CH-2, CH-10)."
    - Also add to M1-AC3's unit cases (:1063): "a stopped turn's result whose totals equal the result before it, built from probe P-11's frames".
    - A design note on the replacement text: keying on the adapter's own stop, rather than on totals equal to the baseline, keeps a real zero-cost turn (a local slash command) from being marked "not reported". In the race where the turn ended just before the stop, it gives "not reported", which is the honest answer, rather than a wrong figure.
